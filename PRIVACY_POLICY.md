# Privacy Policy for ObunaPulse

Last updated: November 20, 2026

ObunaPulse respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the ObunaPulse mobile application.

1. Information We Collect
- Account & Authentication: If you sign in, basic account details (such as email or authentication identifier) may be used to manage your session and sync your data.
- Financial & Subscription Data: Subscription records, income/expense transactions, categories, and currency preferences entered by you are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your cloud backend when authenticated.

2. How We Use Information
- To provide, maintain, and sync your personal subscription and expense tracking data.
- To send scheduled payment reminders and notifications that you enable.

3. Data Security & Sharing
- Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
- We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to third parties.
